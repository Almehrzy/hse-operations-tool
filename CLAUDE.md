## How ACTC replies to a PACE SVN (learned from replies 005-056, 2026-10-14)

The format moved from letters (2024-25), to a "Response to SVN" page with a short header table (2025 to mid-2026), to the current **Closeout Report** (from SVN 044, July 2026). New replies use the Closeout Report:

- Header: "AHMADIAH CONTRACTING & TRADING CO. (ACTC) / Health, Safety & Environment Department", title "Safety, Health and Environment Violation Notice (SVN) Closeout Report (Rev.NN)", then a table: SVN No. (as PACE wrote it, e.g. SVN-AVK-WP.03-056), Action Code (e.g. "C – Needs Immediate Attention"), Project "The Avenues Khobar – External Roads Improvement Works", Contract No. WP-03, Package WP.03, Location, Issued by "PACE Supervision Consultant", PMC / Engineer "JLL", Date Issued, Date Received, Subject, Status ("Submitted for closure"), Revision, Reply Date. Footer: "ACTC HSE – The Avenues Khobar WP.03 | <SVN No.> Close-Out | Page X of Y".
- Sections: 1. Purpose (one paragraph: "This report shows the actions taken by ACTC (WP.03) to close <SVN> (Action Code C), issued by PACE on <date> for <subject> at <location>."), 2. Summary of Violation(s) (what PACE saw, the risk, and what PACE asked for; then Root Cause), 3. Corrective Actions Taken (bullets in past tense; one block per violation when there are several, each ending "Status: Closed."), 4. Preventive / Sustaining Measures (future tense; sometimes a Ref / Action / Responsible / Target Date / Status tracker), 5. Photographic Evidence (5.1 Before: the SVN photo; 5.2 After: photos in a two-column table; 5.3 TBT photo and attendance sheet), 6. Close-Out Statement (summary, then "We kindly request PACE to review the attached evidence and close this SVN."), then the signature table: Prepared by (HSE Engineer) Faizan Mustafa, Reviewed by (HSE Manager) Mahmoud Fawzy, each with date.
- Language: plain, short English sentences (the 055 and 056 style). Dates as "08 October 2026". Photo captions: "Photo N – what it shows, date, place".
- What the replies usually contain: work stopped and hazard removed at once; a named competent person checked the area; TBT with attendance sheet; a worker SVN under the penalty matrix where a person was at fault; references to the HSE Plan section, method statement or risk assessment numbers, and registers (permit register, REG-017 dust log); preventive items such as daily HSE inspection, induction and repeated TBTs, spare stock in the store.
- When ACTC disagrees, the reply stays polite and gives evidence under "ACTC Clarification" (054 cited OSHA 29 CFR 1926.652; 046 cited the approved shift hours letter), and still lists the controls in place.
- When PACE rejects a reply (045, 055): issue Rev.01, add Rev.00 date, review result and Rev.01 date to the header, quote PACE's comments, answer each one, and attach the records asked for as annexes.
- PACE's stated expectations: checklist updates alone are not accepted without physical rectification; photos must be taken after the SVN and be date, time and location stamped.
- Mistakes seen in past replies, to avoid: wrong SVN number inside the text (the 043 reply says 042 in places and its Word file is named 042), placeholder text left in ("sss" in 023, "Insert TBT attendance sheet, if available" in 056), duplicated captions.

## NCR and SOR from the consultant (learned 2026-10-15)

- **NCR** (Non-Compliance Report, `Quick Access\06.1 PACE NCR`): formal; the consultant gives 7 days for remedial work, after which work may be done at the contractor's cost. NCRs usually escalate a repeated SVN (NCR-005 after SVN-16, NCR-006 after SVN-27). Replies: "Response to NCR-..." with corrective actions and future prevention; for repeat issues a CAPA report (5-Why root cause, corrective table C1.., preventive table P1.., 30-day verification of effectiveness), as in the NCR-006 reply. Rejected replies get a Rev.01 answering each consultant comment with a photo record. The NCR-004 Rev.01 PDF (13 Oct 2026) still contains the placeholder "[sleeves / warning tape]"; the user was told.
- **SOR** (Safety Observation Report, `Quick Access\16. SOR PACE`): about 10 observations per report, each with location, category (from a fixed list of about 41 categories, the same list as the weekly/monthly "SOR Trend Analysis" sent to JLL), risk level and close-out time: E immediately, H within 24 h, M within 48 h, L within 72 h. The contractor fills corrective action, close-out photo and closure date per item, either on the consultant's form (021, 023 "Reply.pdf") or in a CLOSEOUT Excel (014-019). Replies are short, one paragraph per item; when ACTC disagrees it says why (area belongs to WP.02, item not applicable).
- In the page, `consultant` documents carry `kind`: `SVN` (default), `NCR` or `SOR`, a `ref` (the consultant's full number) and optional `dueDate`. NCR due date defaults to received + 7 days. SOR documents use `items` instead of `requirements`: `{location, category, risk (E/H/M/L), observation, recommendation, photo}`; each item's due time comes from its risk level counted from `received`. Evidence `req` for an SOR item is the item index. `example-sor-023` is a training copy of SOR-023 with today's date.

## Handling a new PACE SVN

The page's "PACE notices" tab carries each open SVN to site. Collection `consultant` (one document per SVN, written by Claude): `svn`, `no`, `subject`, `location`, `actionCode`, `issued`, `received` (YYYY-MM-DD), `requirements` (short list of what PACE asked for, one line each, English), `pacePhoto` (asset id), `status` (`open` → `ready` set by site when evidence is complete → `replied` → `closed`, both set by Claude), `replyDate`, `replyFile`. Use document id `svn-0NN`. Collection `svnEvidence` (written by site): `notice` (the consultant document id), `req` (requirement index as text, or `tbt`, `attendance`, `other`), `note`, `photo` (asset id), `takenAt` (file date of the photo), `reporter`, `createdAt`. `example: true` marks demo rows (`example-svn-056`); never use them in a reply.

1. **Intake** (the user gives the PACE PDF): read it fully (Read tool), then create `Quick Access\06. PACE SVN\SVN-AVK01-WP.03-0NN <Subject>` and copy the PDF into it. Add the row: `python scripts\svn_register.py NN issued=YYYY-MM-DD received=YYYY-MM-DD location="..." subject="..." action=C status=Open folder="<folder path>"`. The script refuses gaps in numbering. Extract the PACE photo (`python scripts\svn_reply.py photos "<PACE pdf>" "<scratch dir>"`), upload it to the page (`Artifact` publish with `url`, `file_path`, `asset: true`), and write the `consultant` document so site sees the notice.
2. **Evidence**: site supervisors add photos and notes per requirement in the tab. Read `svnEvidence` where `notice` is this SVN, download each photo (`Artifact` read with `path` = asset id), and check that every requirement, the TBT and the attendance sheet have evidence; ask the user for anything missing, including the competent person's name. Flag photos whose `takenAt` is before the SVN date. Do not describe actions that did not happen. Review the evidence the way PACE will before drafting: look at every photo; check it shows the item it is filed under (and the SVN location); compare file hashes, since one photo filed under several items is rejected (PACE wrote "Triplicate picture for one observation" on 052); check the attendance sheet shows date, topic, location and who conducted it; treat bare claims ("ground is solid") as unproven without a named competent person or record. `takenAt` is only the file date: WhatsApp-forwarded photos carry the forwarding date, so rely on the date stamp printed on the photo when there is one. Give the user the gaps in plain Arabic and leave unproven items out of the reply.
3. **Draft**: write `reply.json` (format in the header of `scripts/svn_reply.py`) following the reply style above, then `python scripts\svn_reply.py make reply.json "<SVN folder>\SVN-AVK01-WP.03-0NN <Subject> - Reply.docx"`. It refuses to write if the text names another SVN number, keeps placeholder text, or cites a photo that does not exist. It never overwrites; a revision gets its own file name (`... - Reply Rev.01.docx`).
4. **Review**: the user opens the Word file, edits if needed and saves it as PDF in the same folder (Word automation does not work on this PC).
5. **Register and page**: after sending, `svn_register.py NN reply_date=... rev=Rev.00 status="Reply submitted"` and set the `consultant` document to `status: replied` with `replyDate` and `replyFile`; when the Aconex approval arrives, `svn_register.py NN status=Closed closed=... aconex=<ref>` and `status: closed`.

Template: `templates\SVN closeout template (from 056).docx` (a copy of the 056 reply; header logos and footer come from it). Test output only goes to `test-output\`.
